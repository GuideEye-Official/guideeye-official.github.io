Engineered for Ultra-Low Latency

How Visual Guard processes environmental data to keep users safe in real time.

Android CameraX Pipeline

Captures optimized 720p camera frames and streams memory-efficient compressed payloads directly to the backend.

Claude Vision Parsing

Extracts spatial depth, obstacle coordinates, signs, and hazards into structured JSON responses via Claude 3.5 Sonnet.

Native TTS Audio Cue

Converts structural JSON into natural, low-latency spoken instructions integrated directly with Android TalkBack.

Obstacle Detection

Identifies stairs, doorways, furniture, and hazards with precise spatial mapping and distance estimation.

Instant Text Recognition

Recognizes and reads signs, menus, documents, and labels up to 500 characters with 99% OCR accuracy.

Voice-Controlled Navigation

Control via gestures and voice commands. Call contacts, send messages, and request information hands-free.

Real-Time Processing

Sub-800ms latency ensures instant feedback. All processing happens on your device for maximum privacy.

Visual Support Initiative

Visual Guard is part of the larger Visual Support initiative, dedicated to empowering sight and assistance for the visually impaired community.

With over 285 million people globally living with vision impairment, our mission is to transform smartphones into intelligent navigation companions. We believe accessibility should be affordable, private, and empowering.
